\section*{Supplementary Table S1. Reference Classification}

\subsection*{Legend}

\noindent\textbf{Scope.}
All \Ncited{} sources cited in the revised manuscript: \Nevidence{} evidence studies,
\Nposition{} position papers, \Ncontext{} context sources and \Nlegal{} legal and normative instruments.
The systematic search was conducted on \cutoffdate{} (cutoff).

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Columns.}
\begin{description}
\item[\#] Sequential number (alphabetical by citation key).
\item[Key] BibTeX citation key.
\item[Citation] First author, year and abbreviated title.
\item[Venue] Journal, conference or institution (truncated).
\item[Cat.] Category of use: \textbf{E} evidence study, \textbf{P} position paper, \textbf{C} context or background, \textbf{L} legal or normative instrument.
\item[In orig.] \textbf{yes}: listed in the original submission (\NorigCited{} references; \NorigYes{} retained, \NorigRemoved{} removed with the material they supported); \textbf{no}: added during the revision (\NorigNo{}).
\item[Route] For revision entries: \textbf{S} structured search; \textbf{CH} citation chasing; \textbf{R} added during revision; \textbf{A} anchor study fixed before the search. Original entries show ---.
\item[Anchor] \textbf{yes} if route A.
\item[$\dagger$] No archival peer-reviewed version at the cutoff date (\Nnonarchival{} sources).
\end{description}
